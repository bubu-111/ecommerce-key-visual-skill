# Category and visual-direction reference

This table is for **starting reference only when tone cues are missing**: it is not a scope of application, not a mandatory classification, and not a general compliance list. This Skill applies to any product category — the categories below are merely common examples, and a category not listed here should be analysed from that product's actual purchase decision and declared. The user's brand guidelines, the target audience and the factual material always take priority over this table.

| Common category (examples) | Tone that may be referenced | Information emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food and drink | Warm, fresh, everyday | Taste, ingredients, portion; use only supported descriptions |
| Beauty and personal care | Clean, refined, translucent | Feel, ingredients, benefits backed by evidence provided |
| Apparel, shoes and bags | Simple, lifestyle-oriented | Fabric, cut, styling and true appearance |
| Digital and home appliances | Precise, calm | Performance, specification and test conditions |
| Home and baby | Gentle, orderly | Ease of use, material and applicable conditions |
| Sports and outdoors | Light, durable feel | Usage scenario, craft, proven performance |

Requirements such as licences, certification, age or special statements should be judged from the specific product, the placement and a valid official basis; distinguish what must be shown in the key visual from what is a packaging mark. Do not generalise one requirement across a whole category. Important limiting conditions inside the user's material must be kept.
